# C05 historical data feasibility preflight

**Decision:** the frozen September–October 2024 price study is reproducible, but the retained panel and bounded source checks do not establish all evidence required for strict T1/R1/B1 economic evaluation. This finding is limited to the sources checked here; it does not claim every possible provider was searched. Do not spend another long session reconstructing the same candles and call it strategy evidence. HEAD probes located candidate trade, funding and depth objects, but their contents were not inspected and they are not yet qualified feeds. The retained/probed sources do not establish point-in-time instrument state, validated OI, comprehensive event clearance, exact contemporaneous quotes, or actual fills.

## Executive conclusion

The 112 C04 archives are authentic Binance USDⓈ-M OHLCV for seven declared symbols, four intervals, July–October 2024. They contain 356,454 rows, with complete interval continuity in the committed manifest. They are suitable for price-path and bar-derived quote-turnover research. They do not establish that a symbol was eligible at a historical decision time, what its spread or executable depth was, what the owner would have paid, or whether an IOC would have filled.

The candidate 2024 monthly archive URLs for SOLUSDT and DOGEUSDT returned HTTP 200 to HEAD for aggregate trades and funding; the SOLUSDT `bookDepth` candidate also returned HTTP 200 to HEAD. No ZIP bytes or rows were inspected, so these results establish candidate object availability only—not validated trade, funding or depth observations. Binance’s public-data README describes the trade fields and says daily archives appear the next day while monthly archives are published on the first Monday of the month. That archive timing is later than many decision times inside the month. C04’s previously validated kline records are separate: their quote-volume fields support a bar-derived turnover proxy classified `OBSERVED_WITH_AVAILABILITY_PROXY`, with candle close as an explicit availability proxy. They do not qualify the HEAD-only archives.

The retained panel and probed sources do not establish a complete, point-in-time 2024 universe and filter history, exact historical ticker observations, 60 consecutive minute quote snapshots, validated OI notional, authoritative all-clear news coverage, account-specific fees, or actual execution outcomes. The OI history API is documented as retaining only the latest 30 days. Four candidate metrics paths returned 404 for the selected altcoins; because only candidate paths were tested, this is not proof that no alternate Binance path or third-party source exists. Historical OI remains unverified and does not satisfy C02 from the evidence checked here.

**Historical 2024 feasibility: BLOCKED for strict economic qualification.** Continue with a prospective, public, read-only observation period. Preserve all existing T1/R1/B1 rules, risk limits and holdout policy. Do not promote a C05 economic GO from source existence or synthetic mechanics.

## What C04 already accomplished

The merged C04 manifest records 112 monthly Binance kline ZIPs: seven declared symbols × four intervals × four months. It records 15,417,887 compressed bytes, 356,454 normalized rows, HTTP 200 for all 112 acquisitions, zero ZIP/CSV integrity failures, zero missing intervals, duplicates, or out-of-order opens. The fixed set includes SOLUSDT and DOGEUSDT, but is not a reconstructed historical dynamic universe.

The period was split before replay: July–August source-only warm-up, September development, October 1–14 validation, and October 15–31 untouched evaluation, with a 12-hour purge/embargo. C04’s 1-hour diagnostic replay remained fail-closed: zero shortlist windows, zero strict candidates, and zero strict historical outcomes. Missing reasons included metadata, ticker, spread samples, depth, OI, and event clearance. There were no funding settlements or observed fills in the retained data. The replay was not a full 5-minute execution replay.

Raw archives are specified as living outside Git under `ASTRABOT_DATA_ROOT`. That variable was not configured in this execution, and no ZIP files were found in the checked-in C04 directories. I therefore used the committed manifests and summary only. I did not redownload or replay any of the 112 kline archives.

## Historical feeds not established by the retained and probed sources

- The retained C04 sources do not contain historical listing/status and filter snapshots for all eligible and later inactive symbols. Current `exchangeInfo` is not a historical snapshot; OHLCV presence only proves that trades or bars existed, not that the full contract was eligible under the frozen policy. Other historical records were not searched exhaustively.
- The retained/probed sources do not establish exact rolling ticker or best bid/ask observations. Authentic kline quote volume supports a labeled rolling turnover approximation, not exact ticker or spread.
- Validated historical OI in USDT notional was not established. API history has a 30-day retention limit, tested candidate archive URLs returned 404, and the local C02 contract intentionally leaves OI notional null when units are unvalidated. Alternate archive paths or providers were not ruled out.
- Complete point-in-time event clearance was not established, including the original publication/capture history needed to prove an event was not already known. The event-source space was not exhaustively searched.
- Actual account fees, order latency, queue position, fills, partial fills, cancel races and stop execution tails are absent from the retained C04 evidence; no external account or execution archive was surveyed.

Candidate trade and funding archive objects respond to HEAD, but their ZIP contents remain unverified and cannot yet be counted as recoverable feed rows. A single `bookDepth` response is not proof of complete historical executable depth.


## Risks of misleading backtests

- Treating today’s active symbols or filters as the 2024 universe creates survivorship and invalid-contract bias.
- Assigning event-time availability to a ZIP fetched years later can leak information through archive corrections or delayed publication. C04’s close-time proxy is explicitly a proxy, not original ingestion time.
- Summed candle quote volume can approximate a 24-hour turnover screen; it cannot produce spread, queue position, market impact or IOC fills.
- OI base quantity is not USDT notional without validated contract units and mark-price conversion. Field names do not establish units.
- A settled funding archive records an outcome, not the rate known at entry. Funding cash flows need the held position and the settlement notional.
- T1/R1/B1 outcomes depend on missed entries, partial fills, approval latency, fee tier, funding, adverse stop execution, event vetoes and portfolio competition. OHLCV cannot resolve these.
- Selecting a new 2024 subwindow after looking at outcomes would consume the holdout. A different historic window has the same source defects and cannot be chosen because it looks more favorable.

## Recommended route: C

Build one narrow, unauthenticated, read-only prospective collector for SOLUSDT and DOGEUSDT. Record raw public trades, best bid/ask, bounded depth snapshots, mark/funding and OI observations, current exchange metadata snapshots, source event times, local receive/ingestion times, gaps, and checksums. Keep a separate event-source capture log with original publication and fetch times; do not emit “clear” when required source coverage is missing. Do not add signing, account endpoints, demo keys or order methods. Keep the existing strategies and risk rules frozen.

This preserves the possibility of answering the economic question with future untouched observations. It does not backfill September–October 2024 or qualify C05 today. Paper/synthetic testing remains available for mechanics but is not the selected economic-evidence route.
